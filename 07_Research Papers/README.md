# Aqualytica - Research Literature References

This directory contains research publications, reference papers, and guidelines documents used in the design of **Aqualytica – AI-Powered Water Quality Intelligence Platform**.

---

## Key References

1. **Water Quality Index (WQI) Formulas**:
   - Reference standards used to compute composite scores.
   - Weighted average methods aggregating chemical indicators (pH, TDS, turbidity) compared against relative WHO guidelines.

2. **Machine Learning Model Implementations**:
   - Comparative articles detailing Random Forest algorithms, ensemble tree structures, and Hyperparameter optimizations.

3. **WHO/EPA Drinking Water standards**:
   - Regulatory standards outlining optimal parameter ranges, hazardous thresholds, and environmental water treatment algorithms.
